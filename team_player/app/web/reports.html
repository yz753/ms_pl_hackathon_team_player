Earlier AI reports

Reports describe their original match time, not the current frame. This archive belongs to the open replay and is stored in this browser. Older reports may label derived scores xG: that was legacy terminology for the same calculation, now labelled model units. Historical text is preserved, not regenerated.

Observed movements and model estimates

Movement counts describe observed episodes, not player personalities. Individual and team value are experimental model scores on an expected-goal scale; they are not shot xG or proven effects of a player. Team value is shared with team-mates. These measures do not capture a player's entire performance.

Historical sample and shot-quality comparison

A moment is a sampled window inside a recording, not a whole match. Windows may overlap and are not independent matches. “With movement” and “without movement” are groups of similar past situations, not the same situation replayed both ways. Mean xG is estimated shot quality over the following 15 seconds, not goals or the causal benefit of that movement.
